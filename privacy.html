Privacy

Drybeat · Updated October 31, 2026

The microphone

Drybeat uses the bottom microphone only while you measure the sound or test the microphone, for a few seconds. The sound is analyzed on your iPhone and kept in memory until you leave the screen. Nothing is saved to disk or sent.

What is kept

Your last sound test (date and results) and the number of free sessions used stay on your iPhone, so you can compare. Deleting the app deletes them.

No account, no tracking

Drybeat has no account, no analytics and no advertising.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your name or your payment method.
