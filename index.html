What a sound can do

Sessions play low tones that can help push water out of the speaker grille. They cannot repair a damaged speaker, dry the inside of your iPhone or clear the charging port. If the sound stays muffled, let your iPhone dry and contact Apple Support.

Volume

Sessions are loud by design. Keep the iPhone away from your ears while they play, and never run a speaker session with earbuds in.

Drybeat Full

A single payment unlocks unlimited sessions and the full sound test for as long as the app is available. There is no subscription and nothing renews. To restore it on a new iPhone, open the offer and tap "Restore purchases".
